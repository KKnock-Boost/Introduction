KK Capture · 1×1

One tap from your home screen桌面上，一点就录

KK Capture is a 1×1 widget. Tap it to start recording and tap again to stop — you never have to open the app. KK Capture 是一个 1×1 桌面小组件。点一下开始录音，再点一下结束，全程不用打开 App。

  1. Ready就绪

    Gold waveform. Tap once to start; a short beep and vibration confirm it (not in silent mode).金色声波。点一下开始录音，会有提示音和轻微震动确认（静音模式下不响）。

  2. Recording录音中

    Red waveform. Say it in your own words. Tap again to stop, or it stops by itself after 60 seconds.红色声波。用自己的话说出来。再点一下结束，满 60 秒也会自动停止。

  3. Processing处理中

    A small ring while it transcribes and sorts in the background, then it's back to Ready. Thought of something else? Tap again — the next recording starts right away.右下角出现小圆环，表示正在后台转写和整理，完成后回到就绪。又想到一件事？直接再点，下一条马上开始录。

Add it添加方法 Touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → KKKnockBoost → KK Capture. 长按桌面空白处 → 小组件 → KKKnockBoost → KK Capture。

If a capture can't be processed, a small “!” appears on the icon; retry it from Recent Captures in the app. 如果某条捕捉处理失败，图标上会出现小“!”，可以在 App 的“最近捕捉”里重试。

One tap. Speak. Done.一按。开口。完成。

Then it sorts itself out说完，剩下的自动完成

  1. Transcribed on your phone在手机上转写

    Speech-to-text runs on the phone with Whisper. Your audio is never uploaded.语音转文字由 Whisper 在手机上完成，录音不会上传。

  2. Sorted by AIAI 自动分类

    The text becomes a Todo — with the time you mentioned as its due time — or a Thought. If there's nothing to keep, nothing is created.文字会被整理成待办（你说的时间会记成截止时间）或想法；内容不足以整理时，不会生成任何条目。

  3. Thoughts get researched想法会被研究

    A Thought comes back with a summary, a short write-up, pros and risks, a recommendation and sources.想法会附上摘要、整理好的正文、优点和风险、建议以及参考来源。

  4. Saved on your phone保存在你的手机上

    Results are stored on the phone. As soon as the transcript is ready, you can record the next one.结果保存在手机本地。转写一完成，就可以马上录下一条。

Privacy隐私

Your thoughts stay yours.你的想法始终属于你。

Audio never leaves your phone录音不离开手机

Recording and transcription happen on the device and work offline. Choose Base for speed, or Premium (about 190 MB) for accuracy.录音和转写都在手机上完成，转写可离线进行。可选 Base（更快、更小）或 Premium（约 190 MB，更准确）。

Only text goes out只发送文字

To sort and research a capture, only its transcript is sent to our server and AI providers. The result comes straight back to your phone.为了分类和研究，只会把转写后的文字发送到我们的服务器和 AI 服务，结果直接回到你的手机。

No cloud notebook没有云端笔记本

Your Todos and Thoughts are stored on your phone, not in an online library.待办和想法保存在你的手机上，而不是在线的资料库里。

Editing stays local修改只在本地

Changing, organizing or searching your notes never calls AI.修改、整理和搜索都不会调用 AI。

FAQ常见问题

Questions, answered你可能想知道

Which phones does it work on?支持哪些手机？

Android 7.0 or later. An iPhone version isn't available yet.Android 7.0 及以上。暂时还没有 iPhone 版。

How long can a recording be?一次可以录多久？

Up to 60 seconds. It stops by itself at the limit, or when you tap again.最长 60 秒。到时间会自动停止，也可以随时再点一下结束。

Does it work offline?离线能用吗？

Recording and transcription work offline. Sorting and research need a connection: captures are queued and retried automatically a few times, and if one still fails you can retry it from Recent Captures.录音和转写可以离线完成，分类和研究需要联网。捕捉会先排队并自动重试几次；如果仍然失败，可以在“最近捕捉”里手动重试。

Which languages does it understand?支持哪些语言？

The app is in English and Chinese. Speech recognition detects the language automatically; the Premium model handles mixed Chinese and English better.界面支持中文和英文。语音识别会自动判断语言，Premium 模型对中英混说的识别更好。

What happens to what I say?我说的话会怎么处理？

Each recording becomes one result: a Todo, or a Thought with research. If there isn't enough to work with, nothing is created. The audio itself stays on your phone.每条录音生成一条结果：待办，或者带研究的想法。如果内容不足以整理，就不会生成任何条目。录音本身留在你的手机上。

What is stored on your servers?服务器上会保存什么？

Your account, profile settings and usage. Your Todos and Thoughts live on your phone. So that a retried request isn't processed twice, the server keeps each capture's result for up to 14 days.账号、个人设置和用量信息。待办和想法保存在你的手机上。为了避免请求重试时被重复处理，服务器会把每条捕捉的处理结果保留最多 14 天。
